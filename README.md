# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview & Business Problem
* **Question / Objective**: How can a multi-branch supermarket chain evaluate its regional sales performance, product line profitability, and supply chain cost pressures to maximize net margins?
* **Business Context**: Analyzing end-to-end retail transactions across multiple branches (Alexandria, Cairo, Giza) to identify revenue drivers, operational discrepancies, and seasonal anomalies.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Strategic Recommendations
* **Financial Pressure & Margins**: High vendor costs inflate overall Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) to ~95%, leaving a tight net profit margin of 4.8%. Health & Beauty is operating at a loss (negative margin), as its COGS consistently exceeds total sales revenue.
* **Operational & Supply Chain Discrepancies**: Significant COGS variances exist for identical categories across the three branches (Alexandria, Cairo, Giza). This indicates decentralized procurement, inconsistent localized supplier pricing, or varying delivery terms across regions.
* **Seasonal Trends (February Dip)**: A sharp, sudden decline in gross income and sales occurred across all major product lines during February. Performance fully recovered in March, pointing toward seasonal demand shifts or fewer operational days in February.
* **Strategic Action Plan**:
  * *Procurement Centralization*: Centralize purchasing across all branches and renegotiate unified contracts to reduce overall vendor prices.
  * *Pricing Strategy*: Adjust retail selling prices upwards across selected product lines to absorb rising cost pressures.
  * *Inventory Optimization*: Reduce order quantities and stock levels for unprofitable categories like Health & Beauty to prevent margin erosion.
