# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Performance &
Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview & Business Problem
* **Core Objective**: Evaluate multi-branch
  supermarket sales performance, product line
  profitability, and supply chain cost
  pressures to maximize net margins.
* **Business Scope**: Comprehensive end-to-end
  retail data analysis across branches in
  **Alexandria, Cairo, and Giza** utilizing
  Power BI and Power Query for deep financial
  and operational insights.

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Strategic Insights
* **Financial Pressure & Margins**: 
  * High vendor costs inflate overall Cost
    of Goods Sold (COGS) to **~95%**, leaving
    a tight net profit margin of **4.8%**.
  * **Health & Beauty** operates at a net
    loss (negative margin) as its COGS
    consistently exceeds total sales revenue.
* **Operational & Supply Chain Discrepancies**: 
  * Significant COGS variances exist for
    identical product categories across the
    three branches (Alexandria, Cairo, Giza).
  * This points directly toward decentralized
    procurement, inconsistent localized
    supplier pricing, or varying delivery
    terms across regions.
* **Seasonal Trends (February Dip)**: 
  * A sharp, sudden decline in gross income
    and sales occurred across all major
    product lines during February.
  * Performance fully recovered in March,
    indicating seasonal demand shifts or
    operational calendar differences.

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings & Breakdown

### 1. Commercial Performance & Product Margins
* **Top Revenue Drivers**: *Food & Beverages*
  and *Sports & Travel* lead overall sales
  performance and top-line revenue.
* **Loss-Making Categories**: *Health &
  Beauty* generates the lowest sales while
  facing critical cost pressures leading
  to negative profit margins.

### 2. Branch & Regional Execution
* **Branch Distribution**: Sales volume is
  evenly distributed across branches
  (~32% to 34% share each).
* **Demographic Performance**: Alexandria
  shows higher gross income contribution
  among female customers compared to male
  customers, while Cairo and Giza maintain
  demographic parity.

### 3. Temporal & Seasonal Analysis
* **Sales Volatility**: Total sales dropped
  to ~95K during February before rebounding
  strongly to ~110K in March.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan
1. **Centralize Procurement**: Consolidate
   purchasing across branches to renegotiate
   unified vendor contracts and lower
   overall COGS.
2. **Optimize Pricing Strategy**: Adjust
   retail selling prices upwards on
   low-margin products to absorb increasing
   cost pressures.
3. **Inventory & Category Rationalization**:
   Reduce stock levels or discontinue
   unprofitable items within the *Health &
   Beauty* category to prevent ongoing
   margin erosion.
4. **Seasonal Campaign Planning**: Introduce
   targeted promotions during slow periods
   (like February) to maintain steady cash
   flow and sales momentum.
